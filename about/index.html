About

A ledger, not a review site.

Key Ledger is a static directory for people in the United States who need a replacement remote, flip key, remote-head key, or smart key. Each vehicle is one YAML file. The site renders that file. It does not keep a database, and it does not invent a fact to fill a blank.

What counts as a source

A source is a public page opened on a named date: an equipment-authorization grant, an owner’s guide, a service procedure, or a retailer listing. FCC IDs on this site are taken from grant pages reproduced by FCCID.io when that is what was opened, and the vehicle page says so. Part numbers that appear only on a seller’s page are labeled as seller-listed. A price is the number in that product’s data that day. Stock is taken from a product JSON file or from JSON-LD availability, not from a Sold out label in the theme.

What this site will not publish

  • Star ratings, testimonials, or “customers say” lines.
  • A dealer or locksmith price that was not printed somewhere we can link.
  • An on-board button sequence when the only procedure we found needs a scan tool.
  • A blended procedure when two sources disagree on the timing. The disagreement stays on the page.

Programming steps include a warning that a wrong sequence can leave you needing a locksmith. That warning is required in every vehicle file.

Coverage

The first files are a 2003–2006 Chevrolet Silverado 1500, a 2011–2014 Ford F-150, and a 2012–2016 Honda CR-V. Adding the next vehicle is a new file, described in the README. The FCC ID index rebuilds from those files.
