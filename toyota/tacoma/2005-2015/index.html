2005–2015 Toyota Tacoma

Program it yourself?

2005-2010: a 2006 owner reported an aftermarket remote programmed from the seller's instructions. 2011-2015: the sources conflict.

A 2006 owner reported a $20 aftermarket eBay remote programmed with the seller's instructions. For 2011-2015, KeylessPros says DIY is only for 2004-2010, a Tacoma World member says the fob is self programmable on most 2005-2011 trucks, and some 2011 and 2015 owners report the door sequence worked. A 2015-2018 Toyota manual page prints only Techstream registration.

This page does not apply to 2016 and later trucks, to a truck built without factory keyless entry, or to programming the transponder that starts the engine.

2005-2010

This page does not treat DIY as settled for 2011-2015.

Safety. A wrong sequence can fail to enter programming mode.
Full safety note

A wrong sequence can fail to enter programming mode. Chrome's 2015 base truck never got the locks to cycle, and a dealer told him the truck had no keyless receiver. If the locks do not answer, stop and have a locksmith or dealer check whether the truck was built with keyless entry.

On-board programming

joes06tacoma, a 2006 owner, wrote that a $20 shipped aftermarket eBay remote from keyless king 'Programmed no problem using his instructions.' TRDSport10 wrote that GQ43VT20T is self programmable on most 2005-2011 Tacomas. Yaozer wrote that an OEM fob worked on a 2011. Hook78 wrote that the posted door sequence worked on a 2015 TRD Off-Road. hoffengineering and 565hunter wrote that Amazon remotes worked on 2015 trucks. KeylessPros says DIY self-programming is only available for 2004-2010 Tacoma. A 2015-2018 Toyota manual page prints Techstream registration and no switch sequence. Chrome wrote that a dealer phone quote was about $202 for a fob and programming. That is one person's report of a quote, not a posted price. [1][2][5][3]

  1. Start with the key out of the ignition, the driver door open and unlocked, and the other…

    Start with the key out of the ignition, the driver door open and unlocked, and the other doors closed. This wording is the forum post, not a Toyota manual. [2]

  2. Within 5 seconds, insert the key and remove it twice.[2]
  3. Within 40 seconds, close and open the driver door twice.[2]
  4. Insert the key and remove it once.[2]
  5. Within 40 seconds, close and open the driver door twice again.[2]
  6. Insert the key, close the driver door, and turn the ignition from LOCK to ON and back to…
    More

    Insert the key, close the driver door, and turn the ignition from LOCK to ON and back to LOCK twice, about one second apart, for rewrite mode. Remove the key. The post says the locks cycle twice if rewrite mode was selected. [2]

  7. Within 40 seconds, press Lock and Unlock together for 1 second, then within 3 seconds pr…
    More

    Within 40 seconds, press Lock and Unlock together for 1 second, then within 3 seconds press Lock. The post says one lock cycle means the remote was recognized and two cycles means it was not. [2]

  8. Repeat that button pair for each other remote, up to four.
    More

    Repeat that button pair for each other remote, up to four. Opening the driver door, inserting the key, waiting 40 seconds, or registering four remotes ends the mode. [2]

Fob will not program

The locks never cycle

Chrome wrote that a 2015 base manual truck never entered programming mode. A dealer, and later a master mechanic, told him that truck had no radio hardware for a fob. Defvayne wrote that the door sequence needs the factory receiver.[2]

The locks cycle twice

pheerles's posted steps say two lock cycles mean the remote was not recognized. theferalkid wrote that on a 2015 the locks cycled twice and the remote still worked after another Lock press. Those two reports do not say the same thing.[2]

Which one to buy

Genuine Toyota 3-button remote

  1. The FCC ID on the remote, or the ID the seller states, should be GQ43VT20T. A seller-listed ID is not the grant ID.
  2. The button count should match this file: 3.
  3. The frequency should match this file: 315 MHz.
