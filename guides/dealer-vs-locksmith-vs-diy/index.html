Cost guide

Dealer, locksmith, or do it yourself.

National ranges below are from one Kelley Blue Book article, not from quotes for the Silverado, F-150, or CR-V in this directory. Those three pages keep their own price rows, and several of those rows are still unverified on purpose.

KBB article by Chris Hardesty, dated July 14, 2025. Accessed November 19, 2026.

What KBB published

The article says replacement often requires a dealership and can run from $150 to $500 or more. KBB says it contacted dealers around the country. The quotes it reports are about $200 to $400 for a 2020 Subaru Forester with push-button start, about the same for a 2020 Toyota Sienna, as little as $150 for a 2017 Honda Accord, and roughly $500 for a 2023 BMW X7. It also mentions an Aston Martin crystal fob around $2,000 as an outlier, which is not a useful budget for a normal US car.

On locksmiths, KBB says a reputable shop might offer 10% to 25% savings compared with a dealership, often with mobile service. On DIY, it says an aftermarket fob might be $50 to $100 and an OBD-II programmer about $50, with the risk of a failed programming. Every dealer KBB surveyed required a visit and proof of ownership or registration before programming a fob. Read the KBB article.

How to use that next to a vehicle page

  • 2003–2006 Silverado 1500: the on-board remote sequence is published. Car and Truck Remotes, USD 29.95, in stock, checked November 19, 2026. NorthCoast Keyless, USD 24.94, out of stock, checked November 19, 2026. Standard Auto Part, USD 30.00, in stock, checked November 19, 2026. Amazon.ca lists this package as 1 complete remote key. The CAD 37.00 price is not shown. Dealer and locksmith totals were not found.
  • 2011–2014 F-150: on-board programming needs two working keys, and the published hold times disagree. Car and Truck Remotes, USD 29.95, in stock, checked November 19, 2026, hardware only. Installed prices were not found.
  • 2012–2016 CR-V: service pages require a scan tool. Tom's Key Company, USD 54.95 and USD 60.95, both in stock, checked November 19, 2026. Keys4Less, USD 88.00, out of stock, checked November 19, 2026. Puget Sound Locksmith lists USD 150 for the 2014-2016 4-button key, including cutting and programming at the shop, checked November 19, 2026. That is not a parts-only price. A Honda dealer installed price was not found.

A $30 remote plus a free on-board sequence is a different job from a transponder key that has to be cut and registered with a tool. KBB’s switchblade and smart-key rows are closer to the second job. Do not add the KBB total on top of a hardware price from a vehicle page. They are different measurements.
