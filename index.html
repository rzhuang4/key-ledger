Chevrolet Silverado 1500

The keyless-entry remote on a 2003-2006 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 can be programmed at the ignition, without a scan tool.

On-board programming2011-2014

Ford F-150

A 2013 F-150 owner's guide says you can program your own integrated keyhead transmitter if you already have two programmed keys.

Needs a tool2012-2016

Honda CR-V

Honda service pages for the 2012-2016 CR-V say a Honda Diagnostic System (HDS) scan tool is required to register an immobilizer key, and that the same job programs the keyless transmitter.

On-board programming2005-2015

Toyota Tacoma

The 2005-2015 Tacoma keyless remote that sellers list is a separate 3-button fob, FCC GQ43VT20T, part 89742-AE011.

Toyota Tundra

Toyota service pages opened for 2007, 2009, 2011, 2012, and 2013 Tundras publish a switch sequence for the keyless remote.
