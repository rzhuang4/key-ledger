How shop links work.

No affiliate program is connected yet. Every affiliate parameter in the config is an empty string, so shop links are ordinary URLs and this site does not earn a commission on them.

The parameter for every retailer lives in src/config/affiliates.json. Vehicle files store the product URL only. Filling in a query string, such as an Amazon tag, updates every link for that retailer the next time the site is built. Do not put a leading ? or & in the config. The site adds the right separator.

When a parameter is present, those links are marked rel="sponsored". Research pages do not change their prices or their “unverified” labels because a parameter was filled in. We do not publish ratings of these stores.
